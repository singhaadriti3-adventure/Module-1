Mini Biography: Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam was an Indian aerospace scientist and politician who served as the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007. He was a renowned scientist and played a key role in the development of India's missile technology.

Early Life

Dr. Kalam was born on October 15, 1931, in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, India. He came from a humble background and worked hard to pursue his education in science and engineering.

Note: This is a brief overview of Dr. Kalam's life and achievements.

Important Achievements

Dr. Kalam played a crucial role in the development of India's missile technology, particularly the Agni and Prithvi missiles. He was also instrumental in the development of the Indian space program.

He is known as the Missile Man of India for his contributions to the country's defense technology.
