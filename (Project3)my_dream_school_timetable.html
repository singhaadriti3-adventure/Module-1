School Subject Descriptions

Mathematics
Mathematics is the study of numbers, shapes, and patterns. It helps us solve problems and understand the world around us.
Science
Science is the study of the natural world through observation and experimentation. It helps us understand how things work.
Coding
Coding is the process of creating instructions for computers to follow. It allows us to create software, games, and websites.
Art
Art is the expression of creativity through drawing, painting, and other visual forms. It allows us to communicate ideas and emotions.
